# Sportsbook Me DFS AI — Beta Release Notes

**Version**: v1.1-beta
**Date**: August 2026
**Mode**: Invite-only closed beta (test mode)

---

## What's Included

SB-Me DFS AI is a daily fantasy sports intelligence platform. This beta includes:

- 🏀 NBA DraftKings and FanDuel lineup optimization
- 📊 Player projections and matchup analysis
- 🔍 SB-Me Scout™ — injury, lineup, weather, salary monitoring
- 📈 SB-Me Analyst™ — SB-Me Edge scoring, risk analysis
- 🏗️ SB-Me Builder™ — multi-lineup portfolio generation (up to 150 lineups)
- 🎯 SB-Me Coach™ — contest evaluation, ROI tracking, recommendations
- 🧠 SB-Me AI Assistant™ — intent-routed sports intelligence
- 🎛️ SB-Me Mission Control™ — 12-widget operational dashboard
- 🗺️ SB-Me War Room™ — unified intelligence workspace
- 💳 Stripe billing (test mode) — 4 subscription plans

---

## What's NOT Included

- Live sports data — all data is demo/placeholder
- Only NBA is supported; other sports are placeholders
- Mobile app
- Email verification
- Password reset
- Stripe live mode — all purchases use test cards only

---

## Getting Started

1. Log in with your invited account
2. Explore the Dashboard for projections and lineups
3. Try the Optimizer: enter a slate, choose DK or FD, customize your lineup
4. Visit Mission Control for platform health
5. Use the Assistant to ask sports intelligence questions
6. Subscribe via Billing (test cards only: `4242 4242 4242 4242`)
