## DFS Basics

### What is Daily Fantasy Sports?
DFS lets you draft a virtual team of real players within a salary cap. Your team earns points based on actual player performance. Compete against other users for cash prizes.

### Cash Games vs GPP
- **Cash Games** (50/50s, Double Ups): Consistency wins. Focus on high-floor, reliable players. You only need to beat half the field.
- **GPP** (Guaranteed Prize Pool): Upside wins. Large-field tournaments where top 20-25% cash. You need differentiation and ceiling outcomes.

### Bankroll Management
- Never risk more than 10% of your bankroll on a single slate
- Cash games: 70-80% of your weekly volume
- GPP: 20-30% — these are higher variance
- Track your ROI weekly. If negative for 4+ weeks, reduce stakes

## Contest Selection

### Choosing the Right Contest
- **Beginners**: Start with small-field 50/50s to build confidence
- **Intermediate**: Mix cash games with single-entry GPPs
- **Advanced**: Multi-entry GPPs with 150-max entries (Elite Stack)

### Slate Selection
- Main slate (most games): Most competition, most data
- Late slate (few games): Less data, more variance
- Early slate: Often overlooked, lower competition

## Understanding Projections

### What's a Projection?
An estimate of how many fantasy points a player will score based on historical performance, matchup, minutes, usage rate, and recent form.

### Key Projection Metrics
- **Median**: Most likely outcome
- **Ceiling**: 90th percentile outcome (GPP targets)
- **Floor**: 10th percentile outcome (cash game safety)
- **Value**: Points per dollar of salary
- **Boom/Bust**: Probability of significantly exceeding/falling below projection

## Understanding Ownership

### What is Ownership?
The percentage of DFS entries that include a given player.

### Why It Matters
- **High ownership (chalk)**: Safe, popular plays. Good for cash games.
- **Low ownership (leverage)**: Differentiating plays. Critical for GPP.
- **SB ME Edge™**: A score combining projection, matchup, and ownership leverage.

### Ownership Strategy
- Cash: Don't fade chalk without a strong reason
- GPP: Find 2-3 lower-owned players with similar projections to chalk
- Tournaments: Hard-fade 1-2 popular players to create differentiation

## Building Stacks

### What's a Stack?
Grouping players from the same team to capture correlated scoring (QB+WR in NFL, PG+Center in NBA).

### Why Stack?
- Correlation: If one player has a big game, their teammate likely benefits too
- GPP upside: Stacking increases ceiling variance
- Common stacks: PG+Center (pick-and-roll), PG+SF (fast break), star+value

### Stack Strategy
- **Cash**: Light stacking (2 players). Focus on individual projections.
- **GPP**: Heavy stacking (3-4 players). Target high-total games.
- **150-max**: Multiple stack combinations from different games.

## Using AI Effectively

### How to Ask the AI
- Be specific: "Build my best GPP lineup for DraftKings NBA tonight" rather than "build lineup"
- Use strategy modes: The AI adapts to Cash, Tournament, Nuclear modes
- Ask for alternatives: "Show me 3 pivot options at shooting guard"
- Verify: The AI explains every decision — read the explanations

### AI Limitations
- The AI relies on available data. Stale or missing data degrades confidence.
- Injuries and late-breaking news can change projections. Check Scout alerts.
- The AI provides recommendations, not guarantees. DFS outcomes are uncertain.

### Building Trust with AI
- Start with Cash mode (lower variance) to verify accuracy
- Track AI recommendations over 10+ slates
- Review Coach analytics to see historical ROI
- Adjust preferences as you learn your style
