## Onboarding Flow

### Step 1: Welcome (0:00)
- Black screen → "SPORTBOOK ME" logo fade-in
- Tagline: "Powered by 🧠 SB ME Intelligent AI™"
- "Let's get started" button

### Step 2: Account Creation (0:15)
- Email + password registration
- "Create Account" button
- "Already have an account? Sign In" link

### Step 3: Meet SB ME Intelligent AI (0:30)
- Greeting: "Welcome to your AI-powered DFS platform"
- AI introduction card: "I build lineups, compare players, and explain every recommendation."
- Visual: AI chat bubble with example question

### Step 4: Build First Lineup (1:00)
- "Let's build your first lineup together"
- Auto-populates: "Build my best GPP lineup tonight"
- AI responds with sample lineup + explanation
- User taps "Save Lineup"

### Step 5: AI Chat Introduction (1:30)
- "This is your AI Chat. Ask anything."
- Quick-action chips: Compare Players, Injury Analysis, Slate Summary
- User taps one to see AI respond

### Step 6: Dashboard Tour (2:00)
- Quick highlight of each section:
  - AI Greeting at top
  - Quick Actions grid
  - Subscription status
  - Account info
- "This is your command center"

### Step 7: Subscription Overview (2:30)
- Pro Arena: $39.99/mo — Full projections, 20 lineups, AI Chat
- Elite Stack: $79.99/mo — 150 lineups, Deep analytics, Full AI
- Annual plans: Save with yearly billing
- "Choose your plan when ready"

### Step 8: Complete (3:00)
- "You're ready. SB ME Intelligent AI is here to help."
- "Start Exploring" button → Dashboard
