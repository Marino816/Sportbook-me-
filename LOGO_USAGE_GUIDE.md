# SPORTBOOK ME — Logo Usage Guide

---

## Official Logo

The SPORTBOOK ME logo mark consists of:

- **Text**: "SPORTBOOK ME" in bold italic
- **Accent**: Green (#4ade80) treatment
- **Tagline**: "Powered by 🧠 SB ME Intelligent AI™"

---

## Variants

| Variant | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| Full text "SPORTBOOK ME" | App headers, login screen, marketing |
| Short form "SB ME" | Tab labels, small spaces |
| AI tagline | Footers, loading screens, AI sections |

---

## Clear Space

Maintain minimum 16px clear space around the logo on all sides.

---

## Minimum Size

- Mobile headers: 24px minimum
- Tab labels: 11px minimum
- Footer text: 11px minimum

---

## Background

- Preferred: Black (#000000)
- Acceptable: Dark surface (#1a1a1a)
- Never: Light backgrounds without contrast adjustment

---

## Prohibited

- ❌ Stretching or distorting
- ❌ Changing the color to anything other than white or green
- ❌ Adding effects (shadows, outlines)
- ❌ Rotating
- ❌ Placing on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Using "Sportsbook Me" (lowercase) in branding context
