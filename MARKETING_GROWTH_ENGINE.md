## 1. Brand Messaging Framework

### Brand Positioning Statement
For serious daily fantasy sports players who demand an edge, SPORTBOOK ME is the AI-powered DFS platform that explains every recommendation. Unlike black-box optimizers, our platform is built on 🧠 SB ME Intelligent AI™ — technology that builds lineups, analyzes matchups, and tells you exactly why.

### Mission Statement
To empower every DFS player with AI that explains its decisions, transforming data into confidence.

### Vision Statement
To become the most trusted AI platform in daily fantasy sports — where every player, from beginner to professional, relies on SB ME Intelligent AI to make informed, explainable decisions.

### Value Proposition
**One promise**: Every lineup comes with an explanation.
**One experience**: AI that thinks with you, not for you.
**One result**: Confidence in every contest entry.

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"SPORTBOOK ME is an AI-powered daily fantasy sports platform. Unlike traditional optimizers that give you a lineup with no explanation, our AI — SB ME Intelligent AI — tells you exactly why it chose every player, how confident it is, and what alternatives to consider. It's like having a professional DFS analyst on your phone, 24/7."

### Company Story
SPORTBOOK ME DFS AI LLC was founded on a simple observation: DFS players spend hours researching, comparing, and second-guessing — but existing tools just give you lineups without context. We built 🧠 SB ME Intelligent AI™ to change that. Every recommendation comes with evidence. Every decision is explained. Every player can understand why.

### Founder Story
The founder built SPORTBOOK ME after years of playing DFS and realizing that the tools available were either too complex for beginners or too opaque for serious players. The vision: an AI that doesn't just optimize — it educates. A platform where technology makes you better, not just faster.

## 4. SEO Strategy

### On-Page SEO
- Title tags: "SPORTBOOK ME — {Page Title}"
- Meta descriptions: 150-160 chars, include "AI-powered DFS"
- H1: One per page, include primary keyword
- Alt text: All images described
- Internal linking: Cross-link sport pages, pricing, AI

### Structured Data
- Organization schema
- SoftwareApplication schema
- FAQ schema on help pages
- BreadcrumbList on all pages

### Open Graph & Twitter Cards
```
og:title — SPORTBOOK ME — Powered by SB ME Intelligent AI
og:description — The AI that explains every DFS decision
og:image — sbmedfsai.com/og-image.png
twitter:card — summary_large_image
twitter:site — @sportbookme
```

### Technical SEO
- sitemap.xml: Auto-generated
- robots.txt: Allow all, disallow /admin
- Canonical URLs on all pages
- HTTPS enforced
- Mobile-friendly (responsive)

### Content SEO Strategy
- Weekly slate preview articles (high-volume keywords)
- AI tutorial guides (long-tail educational)
- Sport-specific strategy articles (seasonal)
- Product update announcements (brand)
