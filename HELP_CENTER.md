## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: What is SPORTBOOK ME?**
A: SPORTBOOK ME is an AI-powered daily fantasy sports platform. It uses 🧠 SB ME Intelligent AI™ to build lineups, compare players, and explain every recommendation.

**Q: How is SPORTBOOK ME different?**
A: Every recommendation comes with an explanation — why the AI chose each player, confidence level, supporting data, and alternative options. No black box decisions.

**Q: What sports are supported?**
A: NBA is fully supported. NFL, MLB, and MMA are upcoming.

---

### AI

**Q: What is SB ME Intelligent AI?**
A: It's the flagship AI product of SPORTBOOK ME. It builds lineups, analyzes matchups, compares players, and explains every recommendation in plain English.

**Q: Can I ask the AI anything?**
A: The AI is designed for DFS-related questions: lineups, projections, injuries, strategy, player comparisons, slate summaries. It will guide you back to DFS topics if you go off-track.

**Q: Does the AI remember my preferences?**
A: Yes. Set your preferred sport, contest type, risk tolerance, and salary utilization in AI Preferences. The AI uses these to personalize recommendations.

**Q: Why does the AI explain its decisions?**
A: Because you should understand every lineup choice. Confidence scores, supporting data, and alternatives are always provided.

---

### Lineups

**Q: How do I build a lineup?**
A: Three ways: (1) Dashboard Quick Action, (2) AI Chat — "Build my best lineup", (3) Optimizer tab — select platform, strategy, count.

**Q: DraftKings or FanDuel?**
A: Both are supported. Toggle between them in the Optimizer.

**Q: How many lineups can I build?**
A: Free: 1. Pro Arena: 20. Elite Stack: 150 per slate.

**Q: Can I save lineups?**
A: Yes. Every lineup can be saved, edited, and exported.

---

### Subscription

**Q: What plans are available?**
A: Free (basic), Pro Arena ($39.99/mo), Elite Stack ($79.99/mo). Annual plans save you money.

**Q: How do I upgrade?**
A: Subscription tab → tap a plan → Stripe Checkout → instant access.

**Q: Can I cancel?**
A: Yes. Manage your subscription in the Billing Portal anytime.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Free tier gives you basic access. Upgrade anytime.

---

### Account

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Contact support at qa@sportbookme.ai. Self-service password reset is coming soon.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see your credit card. All communication is HTTPS encrypted.

**Q: Can I use biometric login?**
A: Yes. Face ID and fingerprint login are supported on iOS and Android.

## Getting Started Guide

1. Create your account at sportbookme.com or via the mobile app
2. Log in — you'll see the AI-powered dashboard
3. Meet SB ME Intelligent AI in the AI Chat tab
4. Build your first lineup: tap "Build Best Lineup"
5. Explore the Optimizer for custom builds
6. Set your AI Preferences
7. Upgrade to Pro Arena or Elite Stack when ready

## AI User Guide

- **Ask in plain English**: "Build my best GPP lineup" or "Compare Luka vs Shai"
- **Use strategy modes**: Switch between Cash, Tournament, Single Entry, Nuclear, Bankroll
- **Read explanations**: Every recommendation includes confidence, evidence, alternatives
- **Quick actions**: One-tap prompts for common requests
- **Personalize**: Set preferences in AI Preferences tab

---

## Optimizer Guide

- **Select platform**: DraftKings ($50K cap, 8 slots) or FanDuel ($60K cap, 9 slots)
- **Choose strategy**: Balanced, Aggressive, Contrarian, Stars and Scrubs
- **Set count**: How many unique lineups to generate
- **Build**: One tap to generate
- **Review**: Each lineup shows players, positions, salaries, projections
